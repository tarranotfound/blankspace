# Theme Customization

Blankspace favors high contrast, restrained accents, and readable text.

## Suggested workflow

1. Identify the component you want to change.
2. Locate its config under the repository's component directory.
3. Copy the relevant setting into your local configuration.
4. Reload or restart only the affected application.
5. Keep contrast and font legibility ahead of decorative effects.

Colors and font choices are personal preferences; adapt them to your display and lighting.
